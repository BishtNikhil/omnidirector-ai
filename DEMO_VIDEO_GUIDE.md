# 🎥 OmniDirector AI — 3-Minute Hackathon Demo Video Guide

> **Target Duration:** 2 minutes 30 seconds to 2 minutes 50 seconds (Under 3:00 hard limit)  
> **Recommended Tool:** Loom, OBS Studio, or Windows Game Bar (`Win + G` / `Win + Alt + R`)  
> **Resolution:** 1080p (1920x1080) at 60 FPS  
> **Audio:** Clear microphone voiceover

## 🛠️ Recording Tips for Maximum Impact

1. **Browser Fullscreen:** Press `F11` in Chrome/Edge to hide browser address bars for a clean broadcast studio look.
2. **Audio Setup:** Speak at a steady pace, sound energetic and confident.
3. **Practice Run:** Do one quick 1-minute rehearsal click-through before recording the final take.
4. **Platform Hosting:** Upload the completed video to **YouTube** (Unlisted or Public) or **Loom**, and copy the link for the Cerebral Valley submission portal.

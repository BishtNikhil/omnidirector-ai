🎛️ How to Connect Multiple Cameras in OmniDirector AI

                    
Pro-grade multi-cam broadcasting setup using webcams, screens, and virtual cameras

                

                
            

            

                

                    
1. Camera 1 (Host Prime) — Your Primary Facecam

                    

                        Click "Connect Camera" in the top toolbar to activate your integrated laptop webcam or USB face camera. OmniDirector will perform real-time face tracking to keep you centered in 9:16 vertical reels.
                    

                

                

                    
2. Camera 3 (Screen & HUD) — Live Application / Window

                    

                        Click "Share Screen" in the top toolbar to share your Chrome browser, VS Code, presentation, or gameplay window. The AI Director will automatically cut to your screen when active cursor or visual motion is detected!
                    

                

                

                    
3. Camera 2 & 4 — OBS Virtual Camera, Second USB Cam, or Phone

                    

                        You can connect multiple physical video feeds in Windows:
                        
• OBS Virtual Camera: Start Virtual Camera in OBS to route Discord guests, Zoom, or secondary angles directly into OmniDirector.
                        
• Phone as HD Webcam: Use free tools like Camo or DroidCam to turn your iPhone or Android phone into a studio reaction camera.
                    

                

                

                    
4. Offline Camera Protection — Zero Phantom Cuts

                    

                        Look at the MULTI-CAMERA INGEST MATRIX below the main screen. Each camera tile has a [🟢 LIVE / ⚪ OFFLINE] toggle. 
                        If a camera is not plugged in, toggle it to OFFLINE. The Autonomous AI Director will never cut to an offline camera, staying locked on your live camera with dynamic focal framing!
